# Listening - Bộ câu hỏi mới (bản paraphrase, tránh trùng bản quyền)

> Câu hỏi, các đáp án SAI và transcript đã được viết lại (đổi tình huống, tên gọi, cách diễn đạt) so với bản gốc để tránh trùng bản quyền. **Đáp án ĐÚNG được giữ nguyên nội dung như bản gốc, không thay đổi.**

## Câu 1
**Where will they stay on Saturday night?**
A. At a hostel
B. At friend's house
C. In a cabin

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> There's been a change of plan for our weekend getaway. We'd originally booked the hostel by the coast for Saturday, but it turns out they're completely booked up. We looked into renting a cabin out in the hills instead, but the forecast is calling for heavy rain that day. Fortunately, my friend Sarah said we can crash in her spare room, so we'll be staying at her place instead.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nữ** (không nêu rõ giới tính trong đề, chọn theo cân bằng bộ câu)

---

## Câu 2
**Why doesn't he want to eat at the Italian restaurant?**
A. It is too expensive
B. It is closed today
C. It doesn't take reservations

**Đáp án đúng: A**

> I've been trying to find somewhere for us to grab dinner tomorrow night. The Italian spot by the station gets excellent reviews and they've still got tables open, but the prices turned out to be way more than I was expecting. There's a different place about ten minutes' walk away that's much more affordable, so let's just go there instead.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nam** (câu hỏi dùng "he", người nói là nam)

---

## Câu 3
**How does she usually get to work?**
A. By bus
B. By bicycle
C. By taxi

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> Getting to the office is never a hassle for me. There's a bus that stops right outside my building, though it tends to be packed during the morning rush. Taking a taxi would be quicker, but it works out pretty pricey. Most mornings I just ride my bike since it only takes around twenty minutes and I can skip all the traffic.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nữ** (câu hỏi dùng "she", người nói là nữ)

---

## Câu 4
**What time does the class begin?**
A. 6:15
B. 6:30
C. 6:45

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> Hey, quick message about tonight's workout class. I thought it kicked off at a quarter past six, but I double-checked the schedule and that's actually the kids' session. Our class really starts at half past six. I might get there closer to seven if traffic's bad, so please don't wait around for me outside.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nam** (không nêu rõ giới tính trong đề, chọn theo cân bằng bộ câu)

## Câu 5
**What will she do first?**
A. Go shopping
B. Visit the library
C. Meet her brother

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> I've got quite a packed afternoon ahead of me. My brother asked me to meet up with him at the mall around five, and I also need to pick up a few items while I'm there. But before any of that, I have some books that are overdue, so I'm heading straight to the library once my class wraps up. Only after that will I go meet my brother.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nữ** (câu hỏi dùng "she", người nói là nữ)
