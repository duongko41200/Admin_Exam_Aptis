# Listening - Câu 14-20 (bản paraphrase, tránh trùng bản quyền)

> Câu hỏi, các đáp án SAI và transcript đã được viết lại (đổi tình huống, tên gọi, cách diễn đạt) so với bản gốc để tránh trùng bản quyền. **Đáp án ĐÚNG được giữ nguyên nội dung như bản gốc, không thay đổi.**

## Câu 1
**Where are they going to meet before catching the bus?**
A. At the market place
B. Near the train stop
C. By the old chapel

**Đáp án đúng: A**

> Hi! Are you still at the gym? Let's team up so we can catch the same bus back. Rather than hanging around near the old chapel — it's way too loud there — let's head a little further down the road. I'll see you at the market place, right beside the front gate; it's much easier to notice the bus coming from that spot. See you shortly!

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nam**

## Câu 2
**Where should the visitors gather for the bus?**
A. By the hotel's side entrance
B. At the hotel's main entrance
C. In front of the cinema

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> For this evening's meal, let's gather by the side entrance of the hotel — no bus needed since the restaurant is within walking distance. Around midday tomorrow, though, a coach will pick us up, so please be at the hotel's main entrance. Then tomorrow night, we'll all meet in front of the cinema.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nữ**

## Câu 3
**How are the two of them connected?**
A. Co-workers
B. Best friend
C. Relatives

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> Sharing a home with somebody you truly cherish feels amazing. The two of us rent the same flat during our studies, and even though people often assume we're co-workers, we're really just splitting the rent as students. Folks also sometimes think we're relatives since we live under one roof, and honestly, we do look out for each other — running errands, splitting bills. When I think about it, having a person like that nearby changes everything. She's more than just someone I share a flat with; she's genuinely my best friend, the one who knows everything about me.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nữ**

---

## Câu 4
**Where will everyone gather to catch the bus?**
A. Near the hotel's side entrance
B. At the hotel's main entrance
C. By the concert hall

**Đáp án đúng: B**

> Hi all, a quick note on tomorrow's plan. This evening we'll have our group meal, meeting by the side entrance of the hotel before heading to the restaurant — no bus will be waiting there. Tomorrow morning we're heading out for a lunch excursion, so everybody must be at the hotel's main entrance to catch the bus. Then tomorrow night we'll go to the concert hall for a performance, and again, no bus is required for that. So just to confirm — the main entrance is where we catch the bus tomorrow!

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nam**

## Câu 5
**Which region is currently enjoying the finest weather?**
A. North
B. South
C. East

**Đáp án đúng: C**

> Time for today's regional forecast. Down in the South, expect strong winds and scattered showers, so an umbrella is a must. Over in the North, conditions are pleasant with clear skies and plenty of sun throughout the day. That said, if you're after the very best conditions right now, head East — skies are perfectly clear there with lovely mild temperatures. Meanwhile, in the West, clouds will roll in along with a cool breeze as evening approaches.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nữ**

## Câu 6
**Roughly how many issues of the magazine come out each summer?**
A. Around 150,000 copies
B. About 500,000 copies
C. Just over 300,000 copies

**Đáp án đúng: C**

> Our publication, City Pulse, has expanded quite a bit in recent years, and we now employ around 150 staff members. Print runs vary throughout the year, but summer is when demand peaks. During that period, we put out just over 300,000 copies.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nam**

## Câu 7
**What item did the man leave behind at his friend's place?**
A. His bag
B. His glasses
C. His phone

**Đáp án đúng: A**

> Guess what happened last night! I went round to Tom's place for dinner, and when I got home I realised my bag was missing — I must have left it by his sofa. Good thing my phone and glasses were both in my pocket the whole time, so at least I didn't lose those. I'll swing by tomorrow to pick it up.

**Giọng đọc gợi ý: Nam** (vì câu hỏi xác định người nói là "the man")
